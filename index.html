Provider call script - the spine

Opener (30 sec)

"Hi [Name], this is [Moom/Alex] from Veyya. We're launching Bangkok's first AI-matched premium services marketplace. Got 15 minutes? I want to see if you're a fit."

Discovery (3 min)

  • Specialty + experience · current schedule · mobile-readiness · current customer source · existing certifications · current income

Pitch hook (5 min)

"78% take-home, weekly payouts T+2, free Academy + cert, free pro headshot, free uniform, free background check, insurance included. We bring customers. You stay independent."

Top 5 objections - have responses ready

  1. What's the catch? → 22% take rate, that's it
  2. I already have customers → Veyya is incremental, no exclusivity
  3. Sounds too good - legit? → Bangkok-registered, real founders, come to launch event
  4. Real earnings? → ฿45-65k typical, ฿80k+ Premium tier
  5. What if I fail Academy? → 80% pass first time, one retake, coaching available

Close

"Great. Pack via LINE today. Review 24h. DM me to lock application."

Sin's red-flag list (auto-flag in daily KPI email)

  • Provider no-show rate > 2% in any week
  • Customer 30-day retention < 30%
  • CAC > ฿300 on any channel for 2+ weeks (kill it)
  • Take rate compression (anything < 20%)
  • Provider monthly churn > 12%
  • P0/P1 incident count > 5/month
  • Refund rate > 4% of bookings
  • Reviews mentioning safety / cleanliness / professionalism issues

Any of these → emergency review at next 9am huddle.
